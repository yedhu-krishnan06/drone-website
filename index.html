WHO WE ARE

About us

GYROWING is the drone team of the Federal Institute of Science and Technology (FISAT), Angamaly, Kerala — a student-driven engineering team focused on designing, developing and testing unmanned aerial vehicles.

Bringing together students across multiple engineering disciplines, we turn ideas into working aerial systems. From mechanical design and flight-control software to communication systems and electrical integration, our team works collaboratively to solve real engineering challenges and push the boundaries of UAV technology.

Through hands-on experimentation, iterative design and a passion for aerospace innovation, GYROWING aims to build capable systems, grow technical expertise and represent FISAT with pride.
